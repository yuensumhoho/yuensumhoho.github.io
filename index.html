About me

Welcome to my website.

I'm a pattern cutter, garment developer, and designer. Over the past six years, I have studied fashion in the UK while gaining industry experience in both China and the UK.

I believe fashion is a meaningful way for people to express themselves. Seeing someone wear a garment I helped create—and feeling confident and comfortable in it—is what motivates me most.

I'm both a pattern cutter and a designer. Pattern cutting is not just a technical process for me; it is my design method.

Feel free to explore my work, experience, and the design approach behind each project.
